==========
Python API
==========



Names
-----

The public names live in ``python/cpmdc``. Importing ``cpmdc`` does not import
ASE. Import ``cpmdc.ase`` for ``CPMDCCalculator``.

.. table::

    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | Name                           | Role                                                               |
    +================================+====================================================================+
    | ``HARTREE_TO_EV``              | Hartree to eV, the constant in ``src/cpmdc.c``                     |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``BOHR_TO_ANGSTROM``           | Bohr to Angstrom, the constant in ``src/cpmdc_params.h``           |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``CpmdcError``                 | failure from the library or from loading it                        |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Evaluation``                 | ``energy_h``, ``forces_h_bohr``, ``stress_h_bohr3``                |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session``                    | one ``CPMDCSession`` handle                                        |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session.energy_forces``      | one geometry, native units                                         |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session.version``            | ``cpmdc/`` and the version string                                  |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session.abi_version``        | numeric ABI generation                                             |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session.available``          | true when OpenCPMD is linked                                       |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Session.close``              | destroy the handle                                                 |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Potential``                  | rgpot-shaped provider over one session                             |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Potential.evaluate``         | eV, eV/Angstrom, optional stress                                   |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Potential.caps``             | ``stress`` true, ``energies`` false                                |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``Potential.close``            | close the owned session                                            |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``find_library``               | explicit path, then the three environment variables                |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``find_schema``                | ``CPMDC_SCHEMA`` or ``schema/Potentials.capnp``                    |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``forces_to_ev_per_angstrom``  | Hartree/Bohr forces to eV/Angstrom                                 |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``stress_to_ev_per_angstrom3`` | row-major Hartree/Bohr\ :sup:`3`\ tensor to eV/Angstrom\ :sup:`3`\ |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``CPMDCCalculator``            | ASE calculator: energy, free\ :sub:`energy`\, forces, stress       |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``CPMDCCalculator.calculate``  | fills energy, free\ :sub:`energy`\, forces, and stress             |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``CPMDCCalculator.potential``  | the ``Potential`` the calculator owns                              |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+
    | ``CPMDCCalculator.close``      | close that potential                                               |
    +--------------------------------+--------------------------------------------------------------------+

``free_energy`` on ``CPMDCCalculator`` equals ``energy``. A repeated geometry
returns the stored step. A null or empty input raises; it does not abort
the interpreter.
