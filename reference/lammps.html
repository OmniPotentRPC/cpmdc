LAMMPS interface reference¶

The in-process routes of Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces, cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal and pair_style cpmdc, pass the same numbers to LAMMPS. The Python class adapts cpmdc.Session to the standard rgpot Python potential protocol and delegates its LAMMPS mechanics to rgpot.lammps.FixExternal. The plugin remains cpmdc’s compiled, LAMMPS-specific route. cpmdc-mdi is the separate MDI engine for fix mdi/qm. Its wire units and stress are under MDI engine. This page gives the units, the cell and positions, and the virial.

Units¶

Cell and positions¶

LAMMPS keeps a restricted triclinic box: a along x, b in the xy plane. cpmdc takes one lattice vector per row of a row-major 3 by 3 cell, so the cell sent is

Row

(lx, 0, 0)

with lx = xhi - xlo and the tilt factors xy, xz, yz. An orthogonal box is the case with zero tilts. The positions are LAMMPS’ Cartesian coordinates unwrapped with the image flags, x + ix a + iy b + iz c, so each atom follows a continuous path across the boundaries and wavefunction extrapolation sees smooth steps. The box origin (xlo, ylo, zlo) is not subtracted. A box with all three axes periodic sends the cell; a box with none sends no cell, and the CPMDParams deck sets the box. A box with some axes periodic and others not is refused; FixExternal raises ValueError.

Atom IDs¶

Both routes gather the system and order their call to cpmdc by increasing atom ID. IDs must be positive and unique, but need not be the consecutive integers 1 through N: deleting atoms without compression leaves holes that are preserved while each force is returned to its original atom.

Changing the atoms’ elemental composition, including by delete_atoms ... compress no or fix evaporate, closes the current cpmdc session before the next evaluation and opens a new session for the changed topology. An unchanged composition continues on the existing session.

Electrostatic QM/MM embedding¶

Both routes keep their whole-system behavior by default. For FixExternal, pass qm_types=[...]; other atom types provide their LAMMPS q values as Gaussian MM charges. mm_width_angstrom defaults to 0.30. For the plugin, write NULL for each MM type, for example pair_style cpmdc params.bin width 0.30 followed by pair_coeff * * O H NULL.

Element entries are QM and NULL entries use the per-atom charge. The energy includes the QM–MM electrostatic coupling and forces are returned to both groups. Electrostatic embedding is currently isolated only; a periodic box is rejected instead of using an incomplete image convention, so the virial \(-dE/d(\mathrm{strain})\), which needs a cell, is zero on both routes. qm_types must name at least one type, and mm_width_angstrom and the plugin’s width must be finite and at least 1e-12 Angstrom; a smaller value is refused when the fix or the pair style is configured. Both routes send the charges on every step that has MM atoms, and clear them on the first step that has none after a step that had some, so deleting or retyping every MM atom leaves no stale charge in the session.

Virial and pressure¶

cpmdc returns the virial W = -dE/d(strain) in Hartree. LAMMPS’ global virial of a pair style or of fix_modify virial yes has the same sign and units of energy, W = sum r f for a pair potential, so both in-process routes pass W times the energy factor, symmetrized, as xx yy zz xy xz yz. The pressure LAMMPS prints is then P = (sum m v v + W) / V in its pressure units; a compressed cell has a positive diagonal of W and a positive pressure. fix npt and fix nph use it as they use any pair virial. compute ID all pressure NULL virial reports the virial part alone, W / V in pressure units. LAMMPS accepts only the group all for that compute. The MDI engine does not send this extensive virial. It sends the intensive stress, as MDI engine describes.

The Python session adapter¶

cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal hands rgpot.lammps.FixExternal a small potential that wraps one cpmdc.Session. rgpot passes Angstrom and eV, so the adapter turns them into the atomic units Session.calculate_au takes, and back, with factors it derives once when the driver is built, from the unit factors of rgpot and of this page:

  • Positions and the cell are divided by one factor, the LAMMPS length per Bohr over the length per Angstrom of rgpot. Dividing, as the route before the delegation did, keeps the energy and the forces of metal units bit-identical to that route; multiplying by the reciprocal would not.

  • The energy and the forces are multiplied by the energy per Hartree over the energy per eV of rgpot, and the forces by that factor over the length factor.

  • Forces and virial are views of one buffer, so one finiteness test covers both and the energy. Only after that test fails does the adapter test each array to name the one that holds the non-finite value; the message is the library returned a non-finite energy, forces, or virial.

  • The composition guard compares the sorted atomic numbers as plain integers. A changed composition closes the session and opens a new one, as above.

  • The stress rgpot expects is -W / V in the energy factor. V is the absolute determinant of the 3 by 3 cell, written out as its six products, not a linear algebra call. In metal units the virial agrees with the earlier route to round-off, not bit for bit, because rgpot multiplies the stress by its own determinant of the cell.

rgpot may do per-step work of its own on its side of this protocol. The adapter does not depend on it, and any rgpot.lammps that provides unit_factors, length_per_angstrom and energy_per_ev works.

Per-atom quantities¶

Forces are per atom, and both routes return them for every atom. The energy and the virial are totals of the whole system, and pair_style cpmdc splits neither:

  • A density functional energy is a functional of the electron density, not a sum of terms that belong to atoms, so any per-atom share of it would be a choice of partition, not a result of cpmdc.

  • The virial -dE/d(strain) is a derivative with respect to the cell. It holds kinetic, Hartree, exchange-correlation, and basis-set (Pulay) parts that are not sum r f over atoms, so it has no per-atom form that adds up to the pressure.

pair_style cpmdc therefore refuses a run in which a compute asks for a split: compute pe/atom, compute stress/atom, or compute centroid/stress/atom, with any keywords, even one that is never printed. The error pair_style cpmdc computes no per-atom energy or virial (compute ID) names the compute and comes from init_style, before the first evaluation. uncompute ID removes the request. FixExternal hands LAMMPS one energy and one virial and sets no per-atom values, so compute pe/atom and compute stress/atom take nothing from it. Per-atom forces work with every compute, for example compute ID group-ID property/atom fx fy fz.

MDI engine¶

cpmdc-mdi is the engine LAMMPS fix mdi/qm drives (fix mdi/qm, Using LAMMPS with the MDI library). LAMMPS integrates. The engine is one separate process, not a plugin, and it does not answer fix mdi/qmmm. Build it with the MDI library, as Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces shows. The mdi-qm test checks this contract on the reference evaluator and does not link libmdi.

MDI length is the Bohr and MDI energy is the Hartree. For metal and real, LAMMPS converts with MDI_Conversion_factor before it sends coordinates and after it receives energy, forces, and stress. The engine does not apply the CODATA 2018 factors of the in-process routes. The MDI library’s factors differ from those in the ninth significant figure, so a metal energy from this route is not bit-identical to FixExternal.

nine Hartree/Bohr3 components, row-major xx xy xz yx yy yz zx zy zz

fix mdi/qm copies atom->x and calls domain->remap before it sends >COORDS, so each atom lies inside the box. The image flags are not applied. The engine subtracts >CELL_DISPL from those coordinates before cpmdc_session_calculate_au(), so the positions sit in a cell whose origin is zero. When this step has a cell and a previous step, it then shifts each atom by the lattice vector that brings it nearest to its previous position. A cached geometry, a reused neighbor list, and wavefunction extrapolation therefore see one continuous path, and the energy and forces match the unwrapped trajectory. A displacement of more than half a box edge is that lattice image. A singular cell on a later step fails the evaluation. A new >NATOMS drops that path with the previous cell, origin, species, and coordinates. A step that never received >CELL since the last >NATOMS is a cluster: the session is called with no cell, and no lattice shift is applied. One evaluation serves <PE, <ENERGY, <FORCES, and <STRESS until the next > command.

<STRESS is cpmdc_session_last_stress(), -dE/d(strain) / V, positive under compression. LAMMPS reads indexes 0, 4, and 8 as xx yy zz and the pairs (1, 3), (2, 6), and (5, 7) as the symmetrized xy xz yz, multiplies by the box volume in its length unit, and stores that extensive virial. When the session computed no stress, including an isolated deck and CPMDC_STRESS=0, the nine components are zero. A change in the count of any element closes the session and opens another on the same CPMDParams message. Forces are per atom. The energy and the stress are totals.
