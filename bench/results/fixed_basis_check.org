#+TITLE: A constant number of plane waves from the constant-cutoff functional

With a height A so small that its penalty vanishes, the constant-cutoff
functional gives the energy and stress of the plain deck, and with
~CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE~ above the strain, a cell keeps the plane waves of the
setup it moves from.
Together they give a basis of a constant number of plane waves, whose
effective cutoff falls as the cell grows.
The program below checks both on the one-water deck
~tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt~ (BLYP, 40 Ry) in a 10 Angstrom
cube and in the same cube stretched by 5%, at E0 = 20 Ry, A = 1e-6 Ry and
sigma = 1 Ry against plain setups at 40 Ry and at 40/1.05^2 = 36.281179138322
Ry.

#+begin_src python
"""One water: CONSTANT CUTOFF with A = 1e-6 against a fixed cutoff, at the setup cell and 5% stretched."""
import sys

import numpy as np
from ase import Atoms

from cpmdc import ConstantCutoff
from cpmdc.ase import CPMDCCalculator

WATER = [[0.0, 0.0, 0.1173], [0.0, 0.7572, -0.4692], [0.0, -0.7572, -0.4692]]
params, mode = sys.argv[1], sys.argv[2]
scales = [float(s) for s in sys.argv[3:]]
cc = ConstantCutoff(cutoff_ry=20.0, height_ry=1e-6, width_ry=1.0) if mode == "tiny" else None
with CPMDCCalculator(params, constant_cutoff=cc) as calc:
    for s in scales:
        atoms = Atoms("OHH", positions=WATER, cell=np.diag([10.0] * 3), pbc=True)
        atoms.set_cell(np.diag([10.0 * s] * 3), scale_atoms=True)
        atoms.calc = calc
        e = atoms.get_potential_energy()
        p = -np.trace(atoms.get_stress(voigt=False)) / 3.0 * 1602.1766208
        print(f"RESULT {mode} scale {s:g} energy {e:.6f} eV pressure {p:.4f} kbar", flush=True)
#+end_src

Run on one rank of the OpenCPMD build on FFTW that ran the PBE water
records, with ~python/~ on ~PYTHONPATH~, the CPMD pseudopotentials in
~CPMDC_PSEUDO_DIR~, and ~CPMDC_LIBRARY~ naming that build:

#+begin_src bash
capnp encode schema/Potentials.capnp CPMDParams < tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt > periodic40.bin
sed 's/cutOffRy = 40.0/cutOffRy = 36.281179138322/g' tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt |
  capnp encode schema/Potentials.capnp CPMDParams > periodic36.bin
CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE=0 python fixedpw.py periodic40.bin fixed 1.0 1.05
CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE=0 python fixedpw.py periodic40.bin tiny 1.0 1.05
CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE=0.25 python fixedpw.py periodic40.bin tiny 1.0 1.05
CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE=0 python fixedpw.py periodic36.bin fixed 1.05
#+end_src

Every evaluation ran on an 80^3 real-space mesh.
The plane waves are those that CPMD reports for each setup; the in-place
evaluation makes no setup and keeps those of the one before it:

| Deck | Basis | CPMDC_CELL_IN_PLACE | Scale | Plane waves | Energy, eV | Pressure, kbar |
|------+-------+---------------------+-------+-------------+------------+----------------|
| 40 Ry | fixed | 0 | 1 | 14336 | -459.240469 | -25.4200 |
| 40 Ry | fixed | 0 | 1.05 | 16686 | -459.405051 | -23.5794 |
| 40 Ry | E0 20 Ry, A 1e-6 Ry | 0 | 1 | 14336 | -459.240467 | -25.4200 |
| 40 Ry | E0 20 Ry, A 1e-6 Ry | 0 | 1.05 | 16686 | -459.405048 | -23.5793 |
| 40 Ry | E0 20 Ry, A 1e-6 Ry | 0.25 | 1 | 14336 | -459.240467 | -25.4200 |
| 40 Ry | E0 20 Ry, A 1e-6 Ry | 0.25 | 1.05, in place | 14336 | -456.690914 | -26.1092 |
| 36.281179138322 Ry | fixed | 0 | 1.05 | 14336 | -456.690917 | -26.1094 |

With new setups, the functional at A = 1e-6 Ry gives the energy of the plain
40 Ry setup within 3e-6 eV and its pressure within 1e-4 kbar in both cells.
Moved in place to the stretched cell, it keeps the 14336 plane waves of the
first cell, which a new setup there holds at 36.28 Ry, and gives the energy of
that setup within 3e-6 eV and its pressure within 2e-4 kbar.
