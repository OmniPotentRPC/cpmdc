#+TITLE: Headers of the PBE pseudopotentials

The core radii and the projector cutoffs on the PBE water page are the values in these headers.

* O_MT_PBE.psp and H_MT_PBE.psp
Source: https://github.com/OpenCPMD/pseudopotentials-extended-library/raw/97f98cb25cbee1cce67302addd2bf06e21a17978/pseudo.tar.gz
Paths in the archive: ./pseudo/O_MT_PBE.psp and ./pseudo/H_MT_PBE.psp

SHA-256:
d26bb0bfa19ddaabe95d21169f50fa25915631e84df0d157e93bb32a7a9298ad  O_MT_PBE.psp
8ff874f26491f2b6e482540342ae0ad85c9c7023936f3b050baa4ddced459988  H_MT_PBE.psp

The oxygen report gives rc 1.1200 bohr on 2S, 2P, and 3D.
The hydrogen report gives rc 0.5000 bohr on 1S.
The headers run through the end of &INFO.
