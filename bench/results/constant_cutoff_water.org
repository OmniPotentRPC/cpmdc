#+TITLE: Stress and relaxed volume of one water against the cutoff

~bench/constant_cutoff_scan.py~ generated these tables.
It ran the periodic water deck of ~tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt~
(BLYP, ODIIS, a 10 Angstrom cube) with its two ~cutOffRy~ values set to each
basis cutoff.
It used OpenCPMD ~062582b~ with every patch in ~tools/~, including the
constant-cutoff preconditioner patch, and ASE 3.29.0.
One process ran on an Intel Xeon virtual machine with 8 vCPUs and
~OMP_NUM_THREADS~ unset; no other job ran.
Rows with an E0 use the constant-cutoff functional at E0 = 0.75 times the basis
cutoff, with A = 50 times the basis cutoff and sigma = E0 / 10; rows with ~-~
use the deck as written.

#+begin_src bash
python bench/constant_cutoff_scan.py --library BUILD/libcpmdc.so
#+end_src
