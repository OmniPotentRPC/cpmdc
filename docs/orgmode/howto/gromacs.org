#+TITLE: Run GROMACS on cpmdc forces
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:t

GROMACS can integrate on cpmdc energies and forces in process, for a QM
group that is part of the system or all of it.
~libcpmdc_gromacs~ implements the libcp2k C API that GROMACS' CP2K QM/MM
interface calls, and answers each call with one
~cpmdc_session_calculate_au()~ of the [[file:../reference/c-abi.org][C ABI]].
GROMACS links it in place of CP2K, so ~mdrun~ needs no extra process and no
socket.
Energies and forces need no change to GROMACS' source; the QM virial, and
so the pressure and NPT, needs a short patch to GROMACS' QM/MM module (see
[[*Pressure and NPT][Pressure and NPT]]).

| Route | GROMACS side | Needs |
|-------+--------------+-------|
| ~libcpmdc_gromacs~ | the CP2K QM/MM interface, ~qmmm-cp2k-*~ mdp options | GROMACS 2024 or later built with ~-DGMX_DOUBLE=ON -DGMX_CP2K=ON~ against cpmdc's ~libcp2k.pc~ |

The interface was checked with GROMACS 2024.6, 2025.5, and 2026.4; the
three call the same libcp2k functions, and 2026 adds
~cp2k_get_positions~ and ~cp2k_get_version~, which the library provides.
The MPI build, the installed library, the OpenCPMD backend, and the
virial patch were checked with 2025.5; the 2024.6 and 2026.4 patches apply
and their QM/MM sources compile.

* Why this route
GROMACS offers four ways to bring outside forces into ~mdrun~.
The choice was made from the GROMACS 2024 to 2026 sources and manuals and
the MiMiC papers:

| Route | Coupling | What rules it in or out |
|-------+----------+--------------------------|
| CP2K QM/MM interface (~GMX_CP2K~) | in process, one C call per step | GROMACS integrates; energy enters ~Quantum En.~ (~F_EQM~) and so the potential and the conserved energy; positions, box, and forces in double precision in a double build. Chosen |
| MiMiC (~GMX_MIMIC~, ~integrator = mimic~) | two MPI programs over the MiMiC communication library | built for CPMD, but CPMD is the server that integrates and GROMACS a client that returns MM forces (~src/gromacs/mimic/communicator.cpp~); electrostatic embedding is computed against CPMD's density on its grid. A MiMiC client of cpmdc would need CPMD's integrator and MiMiC's embedding inside a session, which the C ABI does not expose, and GROMACS' ~mimic~ integrator reports no kinetic or total energy |
| neural-network potential (~GMX_NNPOT~, 2025 and later) | in process, a TorchScript model | ~src/gromacs/applied_forces/nnpot/torchmodel.cpp~ casts positions and box to ~float32~, so no model can match a double reference to 1e-10; it adds no virial |
| PLUMED (native in 2025) | in process | the bias forces and virial reach GROMACS, but ~plumedforceprovider.cpp~ never adds the bias energy to GROMACS' energy terms, so the potential and NVE checks would miss it |
| i-PI | socket | GROMACS has no i-PI client |

MiMiC couples CPMD to GROMACS with electrostatic embedding and scales to
thousands of cores [1, 2], through the MiMiC communication library [3];
it is the route for an MPI-launched CPMD that integrates.
For GROMACS integrating on cpmdc forces, the CP2K interface is the one
in-process route that carries the energy, the forces, and the box at full
precision, and it is the one built and tested here.

* Build
Meson builds ~libcpmdc_gromacs.so~ with ~libcpmdc~
(~-Dwith_gromacs_shim=auto~, the default; it is off for the Python wheel
build) and writes ~build/tools/gromacs/pkgconfig/libcp2k.pc~, which points
GROMACS at the library and at ~tools/gromacs/libcp2k.h~.
~meson install~ puts the library in ~LIBDIR~, the header in
~include/cpmdc/gromacs~, and the ~.pc~ file in
~LIBDIR/cpmdc/gromacs/pkgconfig~, apart from cpmdc's own ~cpmdc.pc~, so
that ~libcp2k~ is found only where asked for.

GROMACS' ~cmake/gmxManageCP2K.cmake~ requires ~CP2K_DIR~, prepends
~CP2K_DIR/pkgconfig~ to ~PKG_CONFIG_PATH~, and reads ~libcp2k.pc~ there, so
pass the directory above ~pkgconfig~.
The ~patch~ line adds the QM virial; ~qmmm-virial-2024.patch~ is for 2024.6
and 2025.5, ~qmmm-virial-2026.patch~ for 2026.4.
Without it GROMACS builds and runs NVE and NVT as before:

#+begin_src bash
meson setup build && meson compile -C build
patch -p1 -d gromacs-2025.5 < tools/gromacs/patches/qmmm-virial-2024.patch
cmake -S gromacs-2025.5 -B build-gmx -G Ninja -DGMX_DOUBLE=ON -DGMX_MPI=OFF \
  -DGMX_CP2K=ON -DCP2K_DIR=$PWD/build/tools/gromacs
cmake --build build-gmx --target install
#+end_src

From an installed cpmdc:

#+begin_src bash
meson setup build --prefix=$PWD/build/prefix && meson compile -C build
meson install -C build
cp2k_dir=$(dirname "$(dirname "$(find build/prefix -path '*/cpmdc/gromacs/pkgconfig/libcp2k.pc')")")
patch -p1 -d gromacs-2025.5 < tools/gromacs/patches/qmmm-virial-2024.patch
cmake -S gromacs-2025.5 -B build-gmx -G Ninja -DGMX_DOUBLE=ON -DGMX_MPI=OFF \
  -DGMX_CP2K=ON -DCP2K_DIR=$cp2k_dir
#+end_src

The installed ~libcp2k.pc~ gives ~-L~, ~-Wl,-rpath~ and the two libraries
in ~Libs~, and ~-I~ for ~include/cpmdc/gromacs~ in ~Cflags~, so any
pkg-config consumer compiles, links and runs against it with no further
flags.
GROMACS takes only the library names, directories, and include
directories from it, not the ~-Wl,-rpath~; it finds the library at run time
because it records the directories it linked from as its run path
(~CMAKE_INSTALL_RPATH_USE_LINK_PATH~).
Either way ~libgromacs~ names the build tree or the prefix it was built
against, and a GROMACS built against one does not follow the library to
the other.

~scripts/ci/build_gromacs_cpmdc.sh VERSION PREFIX CP2K_DIR [thread-mpi|mpi]~
downloads a pinned GROMACS release, checks its SHA-256, applies the virial
patch for that release, builds it this way
(~mpi~: ~-DGMX_MPI=ON~ with ~mpicc~, ~mpicxx~ and ~mpifort~ from ~PATH~),
and checks that ~libgromacs~ resolves ~libcpmdc_gromacs~ to the ~libdir~
of that ~libcp2k.pc~.

With OpenCPMD linked, GROMACS must use the BLAS and LAPACK ~libcpmdc~
links: GROMACS' bundled ones (~-DGMX_EXTERNAL_BLAS=OFF~) export ~dgemm_~
and the rest from ~libgromacs~, OpenCPMD's calls bind to them, and its
nonlocal pseudopotential forces come out wrong (see
[[file:troubleshooting.org][Troubleshooting]]).
The script passes ~-DGMX_EXTERNAL_BLAS=ON -DGMX_EXTERNAL_LAPACK=ON~ and the
BLAS of ~libcpmdc~ when ~libcpmdc~ links one, and ~libcpmdc_gromacs~ stops
~mdrun~ when it finds ~dgemm_~ in ~libgromacs~ with OpenCPMD linked.

Build GROMACS in double precision.
Its QM/MM module stores cpmdc's forces as ~real~, which is ~float~ in a
mixed-precision build, and the 1e-10 agreement below holds only for
~gmx_d~.

* Set up a run
The QM group is an index group; ~qmmm-cp2k-qmmethod = INPUT~ makes grompp
take the cpmdc settings from the file of ~-qmi~:

#+begin_src text
qmmm-cp2k-active      = true
qmmm-cp2k-qmgroup     = QM
qmmm-cp2k-qmmethod    = INPUT
qmmm-cp2k-qmcharge    = 0
qmmm-cp2k-qmmultiplicity = 1
#+end_src

~cpmdc.gromacs.qmi_input()~ writes that file:

#+begin_src python
from cpmdc.gromacs import write_qmi

write_qmi("cpmdc.inp", "water.params.bin", embedding="MECHANICAL",
          qm_charges=[-0.834, 0.417, 0.417])
#+end_src

#+begin_src bash
gmx_d grompp -f md.mdp -c conf.gro -p topol.top -n index.ndx -qmi cpmdc.inp -o md.tpr
gmx_d mdrun -deffnm md -ntmpi 1
#+end_src

The file is CP2K input in form, because grompp checks it for ~CHARGE~,
~MULTIPLICITY~, ~RUN_TYPE ENERGY_FORCE~, ~COORD_FILE_FORMAT PDB~,
~CHARGE_EXTENDED TRUE~, and ~COORD_FILE_NAME~ and refuses ~@INCLUDE~.
GROMACS stores it in the ~.tpr~, writes it out at ~mdrun~ as
~md_cp2k.inp~ with the name of the PDB file it generates, and passes both
to the library.
The PDB file gives each atom's element, whether it is QM or MM, and each MM
atom's charge.
The cpmdc settings are a ~&CPMDC~ section inside ~&FORCE_EVAL~:

| Key | Meaning |
|-----+---------|
| ~PARAMS path~ | the ~CPMDParams~ message; a relative path is relative to ~mdrun~'s directory. Required |
| ~CELL PERIODIC~ | send the GROMACS box as the cell; the default when every atom is QM |
| ~CELL NONE~ | send no cell, so the deck's cell holds the QM region; the default with MM atoms |
| ~EMBEDDING MECHANICAL~ | add the QM-MM Coulomb energy and forces of ~QM_CHARGES~ and the MM charges |
| ~EMBEDDING NONE~ | add nothing; allowed only when no MM atom carries a charge |
| ~QM_CHARGES q ...~ | one classical charge per QM atom, in index-group order; required by ~MECHANICAL~ |
| ~EWALD_RCUT r~ | real-space cutoff of the QM-MM Ewald sum in nm; default the smaller of 1 nm and half the smallest box height |
| ~EWALD_PRECISION p~ | ~erfc(alpha rcut) = p~, and the reciprocal cutoff ~2 alpha sqrt(-ln p)~; default 1e-8 |
| ~QM_RANKS ALL~ | under an MPI GROMACS, every rank of ~mdrun~ takes part in one cpmdc calculator; the default |
| ~QM_RANKS 1~ | rank 0 alone runs the QM calculation, the other ranks wait for its result |

Everything outside ~&CPMDC~ is checked and otherwise ignored: the method
is the ~CPMDParams~ deck, not CP2K's ~&DFT~.
GROMACS' generated input (~qmmethod = PBE~ or ~BLYP~) has no ~&CPMDC~
section and is refused.

The library writes one log, the CP2K output file GROMACS names
(~md_cp2k.out~): the cpmdc version, the atom counts, the cell and
embedding modes, the number of ranks and of QM ranks, the Ewald parameters
in Bohr, and, for the first step, the backend (~opencpmd~, or ~reference~
for the evaluator of a build without OpenCPMD) and the QM and QM-MM
energies.
A refused input or a failed step prints ~cpmdc-gromacs:~ and the reason to
stderr and to that log and stops ~mdrun~ with status 1, because the libcp2k
API returns no errors.

* Units
cpmdc works in Bohr and Hartree; GROMACS in nm, kJ/mol, and ps.
GROMACS' QM/MM module converts with its own constants from
~src/gromacs/math/units.h~ (CODATA 2018), so the library receives Bohr and
returns Hartree and Hartree/Bohr.
~cpmdc.gromacs~ reproduces the factors bit for bit:

| Quantity | GROMACS | cpmdc | Factor |
|----------+---------+-------+--------|
| length | nm | Bohr | ~BOHR_TO_NM~ = 0.0529177210903 |
| energy | kJ/mol | Hartree | ~HARTREE_TO_KJ_PER_MOL~ = 2625.4996394798673 |
| force | kJ/(mol nm) | Hartree/Bohr | ~HARTREE_PER_BOHR_TO_KJ_PER_MOL_NM~ = 49614.752589206466 |
| virial | kJ/mol, ~-0.5 sum r (x) f~ | Hartree, stress times volume | -0.5 ~HARTREE_TO_KJ_PER_MOL~ |
| QM-MM Coulomb | ~ONE_4PI_EPS0~ kJ nm/(mol e^2) | e^2/Bohr = 1 | the charges are used as they are |

* Cell and positions
GROMACS sends every atom, QM and MM, each step, after putting each into
the box.
GROMACS 2024 and 2025 send the box as ~box_d[3 i + j] = box[j][i]~, and
GROMACS 2026 as ~box_d[3 i + j] = box[i][j]~, one lattice vector after the
other, which is CP2K's layout.
GROMACS' box is lower triangular, so the two differ only in where the zeros
are, and the library reads either back as one lattice vector per row, the
layout of ~cpmdc_session_calculate_au()~; any triclinic shape GROMACS
accepts passes through.

A wrapped atom jumps by a lattice vector.
The library unwraps the QM atoms against their positions of the previous
step, nearest image, so each follows a continuous path and wavefunction
extrapolation sees smooth steps.
With ~CELL NONE~ the first step also puts every QM atom at the nearest image
of the first one, so the QM region starts whole.
With ~CELL NONE~ cpmdc moves the QM region to the middle of the deck's
cell on every step, by its centre of mass, and removes the net force the
cell would leave (~CPMDC_RECENTER~ in
[[file:../reference/environment.org][environment variables]]), so the QM
energy and forces do not depend on where the region has diffused to.
The cell must still hold the region with room for its density;
~CPMDC_RECENTER_MARGIN~ makes a step fail when an atom comes closer to a
face than that.

* Embedding
GROMACS' CP2K interface assumes electrostatic embedding: grompp zeroes the
charges of the QM atoms, removes the bonds, angles, and dihedrals within
the QM group, excludes QM-QM Lennard-Jones, and keeps QM-MM Lennard-Jones
and the bonded terms that cross the boundary.
The QM-MM Coulomb interaction is then the QM program's job [4].

~libcpmdc_gromacs~ provides mechanical embedding.
~EMBEDDING MECHANICAL~ adds the Coulomb energy of fixed point charges on the
QM atoms, ~QM_CHARGES~, with the MM charges of the PDB file, and its forces
on both sides:

- a periodic box: an Ewald sum with the conventions of GROMACS' PME, the
  real-space term ~erfc(alpha r)/r~ over every image within ~rcut~, the
  reciprocal term over ~k~ up to the cutoff above, and the neutralizing
  background ~-pi Q_QM Q_MM / (V alpha^2)~ of a non-neutral QM or MM part;
  the QM-QM and MM-MM sums are not included, since GROMACS' PME already has
  MM-MM and the QM charges are zero in GROMACS;
- no box: the direct sum.

The returned energy is the QM energy plus that Coulomb energy; both appear
in ~Quantum En.~.
An MD step adds ~O(N_k (N_QM + N_MM))~ work for the reciprocal sum.

Not implemented:

- electrostatic embedding: the MM charges do not polarize the QM density.
  ~cpmdc_session_calculate_au()~ takes no external potential, so
  ~EMBEDDING ELECTROSTATIC~ is refused;
- link atoms across a QM-MM covalent bond; ~&LINK~ sections are refused;
- a relative dielectric constant other than 1 in the QM-MM sum;
- PDB charges have six decimals, the precision of GROMACS' file.

* Pressure and NPT
NPT works with a GROMACS patched as in [[*Build][Build]].
The QM virial is the full tensor, in a cubic or triclinic box, so
C-rescale, Parrinello-Rahman, and the other barostats see the QM part of
the pressure whatever coupling type they use; the runs in
[[*OpenCPMD in GROMACS][OpenCPMD in GROMACS]] use isotropic C-rescale and
Parrinello-Rahman.

GROMACS adds the QM/MM forces through its ~ForceWithVirial~ buffer, whose
forces enter the virial only through ~addVirialContribution()~, and the
released QM/MM module never calls it (the GROMACS manual notes the missing
virial [4]), so an unpatched GROMACS reports a pressure without the QM and
QM-MM terms.
The patch makes the module call libcp2k's ~cp2k_get_stress_tensor()~ on
each step that needs the virial, on the main rank only, since GROMACS sums
the virial over ranks, and add

#+begin_example
Xi = -0.5 * sigma * V * HARTREE_TO_KJ_PER_MOL
#+end_example

in kJ/mol, GROMACS' ~-0.5 sum r (x) f~ convention.
~sigma~ is CP2K's stress, column-major, in Hartree/Bohr^3, positive under
compression; ~sigma V~ is the virial ~W = -dE/d(strain)~ of the
[[file:../reference/c-abi.org][C ABI]] and ASE's stress is ~-sigma~.
~V~ is the box volume in Bohr^3.
With no kinetic energy, GROMACS' pressure is then ~sigma~.

~libcpmdc_gromacs~ returns the strain derivative of the energy it returned,
for a symmetric strain that moves every atom with the box:

- ~CELL PERIODIC~: cpmdc's stress for the cell, from
  ~cpmdc_session_calculate_au()~;
- ~CELL NONE~: the symmetric part of ~sum x (x) f~ over the QM atoms. The
  QM forces sum to zero, since cpmdc removes the net force of the isolated
  cell, so the sum does not depend on the origin of ~x~. Without that
  (~CPMDC_RECENTER=off~) a QM region at ~X~ with net force ~F~ adds
  ~X (x) F~, which grows as the region drifts and as the barostat scales
  it away from the origin. The grid still leaves a small torque, and a
  symmetric strain does not see the antisymmetric part;
- ~EMBEDDING MECHANICAL~ with a box: plus the strain derivative of the
  QM-MM Ewald sum, its real-space, reciprocal, and background terms.

It reports the stress as unavailable with no box (~pbc = no~), and with
~CELL PERIODIC~ when cpmdc computes no stress: an isolated deck, or
~CPMDC_STRESS=0~.
The patched GROMACS then writes once to ~md.log~ that the QM program
returned no stress tensor, and the pressure lacks the QM terms;
~md_cp2k.out~ logs the outcome of the first request.

An NPT mdp adds the barostat to the QM/MM settings above, for example:

#+begin_src text
integrator       = md
tcoupl           = v-rescale
tc-grps          = System
tau-t            = 0.1
ref-t            = 300
pcoupl           = C-rescale
pcoupltype       = isotropic
tau-p            = 0.5
ref-p            = 1.0
compressibility  = 4.5e-5
#+end_src

Two cautions:

- with ~CELL PERIODIC~, each new box is a new setup of the deck at its
  cutoff, so the plane-wave basis changes with the box. OpenCPMD's stress is
  the derivative at a fixed basis and has no Pulay correction, so converge
  the cutoff for the stress, not only the energy, before a periodic NPT run;
- with ~CELL NONE~ the deck's cell has to hold the QM region and its
  density, not the region's path: cpmdc centres the region in it on each
  step.

* Several ranks
A thread-MPI GROMACS runs QM/MM on one rank, ~-ntmpi 1~, and calls
~cp2k_create_force_env~; GROMACS refuses more thread-MPI ranks with QM/MM.

A GROMACS built with ~-DGMX_MPI=ON~ calls ~cp2k_create_force_env_comm~ on
every rank, with the Fortran handle of its simulation communicator, and
then each step sends every rank the positions of all atoms and reads the
forces of its home atoms back.
GROMACS refuses separate PME ranks with QM/MM, so every rank of that
communicator makes each call and the library can use collectives on it.
~libcpmdc_gromacs~ is built with MPI when Meson finds MPI for C, the same
MPI GROMACS is built with, and then:

- duplicates the communicator, so cpmdc's traffic and error handler stay
  off GROMACS' own;
- reads the input and the PDB file on rank 0 and broadcasts the atom
  counts, the QM atoms, and the ~CPMDParams~ message;
- hands the duplicate to cpmdc with ~cpmdc_adopt_calculator_comm()~ before
  the first session, with every rank as one calculator (~QM_RANKS ALL~), or
  ~MPI_COMM_SELF~ on rank 0 (~QM_RANKS 1~). It does so on one rank too:
  GROMACS has started MPI, and OpenCPMD sets up a communicator only when it
  starts MPI itself;
- each step broadcasts rank 0's positions and box, so every QM rank
  evaluates identical input whatever GROMACS' decomposition, adds the
  embedding on rank 0, and broadcasts rank 0's energy and forces.

The forces GROMACS reads are therefore identical on every rank, and domain
decomposition changes which rank applies a force, not its value, including
for QM molecules whose atoms sit in different domains.
With ~QM_RANKS 1~, or the reference evaluator, the QM forces are those of a
one-rank run; with OpenCPMD on several ranks they differ from them by the
order of its parallel sums (see [[*OpenCPMD in GROMACS][OpenCPMD in GROMACS]]).
GROMACS reads the energy on its main rank only, so ~Quantum En.~ counts it
once.

A library built without MPI stops a ~cp2k_create_force_env_comm~ call with
the reason.

* Checks
~tests/python/test_gromacs.py~ runs under Meson's ~python-pytest~.
Without GROMACS it still checks the library through ~ctypes~:

- the unit factors against GROMACS' ~units.h~;
- the energy and forces of the whole system against ~CPMDCCalculator~ to
  1e-10, in a cubic and a triclinic box;
- unwrapping across the boundary, and the whole QM region with ~CELL NONE~;
- the mechanical embedding against an independent NumPy Ewald sum in a
  cubic and a triclinic box, its convergence with the precision, a finite
  difference of its forces, and the direct sum without a box;
- the stress of ~cp2k_get_stress_tensor()~, as ASE's stress: with
  ~CELL PERIODIC~ against ~CPMDCCalculator~ to 1e-13 and ASE's central
  difference under a 1e-6 strain of the cell to 1e-8 of its largest
  element; with ~CELL NONE~, with and without the mechanical embedding,
  against the same difference of the library's own energy; each in a cubic
  and a triclinic box, and the box of GROMACS 2026 read the same as that of
  2024;
- no stress without a box or for an isolated periodic deck;
- the error for each refused input.

With ~gmx_d~ from ~CPMDC_GMX~ or on ~PATH~, it also runs ~grompp~ and
~mdrun~:

- one step of two QM waters, the whole system, in a cubic and a triclinic
  box: ~Quantum En.~ against ~CPMDCCalculator~ to 1e-10 relative and equal
  to ~Potential~, and the forces to 1e-10 relative, read with
  ~gmx energy -dp~ and from the double-precision ~.trr~;
- one QM water among three MM waters with PME: ~Quantum En.~ and the QM
  atom forces against the reference evaluator plus the NumPy Ewald sum;
- 2000 NVE steps of each system: the total energy stays within 2e-3 and
  5e-3 of the swing of the potential energy;
- the virial and pressure of step 0 from rest with ~md-vv~, two QM waters
  in a cubic and a triclinic box: ~Vir~ equals ~0.5 V sigma~ of
  ~CPMDCCalculator~'s ASE stress to 1e-9 relative, and ~Pres~ equals
  ~-sigma~;
- one QM water among three MM waters with PME, in a cubic and a triclinic
  box: each element of ~Vir~ equals half the central difference of
  ~Potential~ under a 1e-5 strain of the box and positions, to 1e-8 of its
  largest element, so the QM, QM-MM, and classical terms all enter it;
- generated input, ~EMBEDDING ELECTROSTATIC~, and charged MM atoms without
  an embedding each stop ~mdrun~ with the reason.

With ~gmx_mpi_d~ from ~CPMDC_GMX_MPI~, a double-precision GROMACS built
with ~-DGMX_MPI=ON~, launched by ~CPMDC_MPIEXEC~ (default ~mpiexec~):

- 20 NVE steps of 64 waters in a 2.4 nm box, one of them QM with its atoms
  across the periodic boundary in x and y, on 2 and 4 ranks with
  ~QM_RANKS ALL~ and on 4 with ~QM_RANKS 1~: ~Quantum En.~, ~Potential~
  and ~Total Energy~ of every step and the positions and forces of steps 0,
  10 and 20 match the one-rank run to 1e-10, and the virial and pressure
  of every step to 1e-9 of their largest element, so the QM virial enters
  once. The pair list is rebuilt every step without a buffer
  (~nstlist = 1~, ~verlet-buffer-tolerance = -1~), so that every
  decomposition sees the same pairs;
- ~-npme 1~ stops ~mdrun~ with GROMACS' refusal of separate PME ranks.

~tests/test_installed_pkgconfig_consumer.py~ builds a libcp2k client from an
installed ~libcp2k.pc~ alone and runs it without ~LD_LIBRARY_PATH~.

The CI job ~gromacs-qmmm~ builds GROMACS 2025.5 three ways and fails if any
of its tests skips:

| Route | GROMACS | Tests |
|-------+---------+-------|
| ~build~ | thread-MPI, against ~build/tools/gromacs~ | the one-rank tests and the Meson suite |
| ~installed~ | thread-MPI, against the ~libcp2k.pc~ of ~meson install~ into ~build/prefix~ | the same, with ~libcpmdc~ and ~libcpmdc_gromacs~ from the prefix |
| ~mpi~ | ~-DGMX_MPI=ON~ with the Open MPI of the pixi environment | the domain decomposition tests |

The reference evaluator of a build without OpenCPMD is a well about the
origin.
Two consequences show in the tests: the mdp sets ~comm-mode = None~,
because removing the centre-of-mass motion of a system with a net external
force takes energy out of NVE, and the QM/MM NVE run gives the QM hydrogens
a small Lennard-Jones repulsion, because the well does not bind them to
their oxygen.
An OpenCPMD energy needs neither.

* OpenCPMD in GROMACS
~bench/gromacs_opencpmd.py~ runs GROMACS on the OpenCPMD backend: one BLYP
water (the deck of ~tests/water_blyp_params.capnp.txt~, 50 Ry, Hockney,
orbital convergence 1e-7) at the centre of the deck's 1 nm cell, among 26
flexible TIP3P waters in a 1.8 nm box, with ~CELL NONE~, mechanical
embedding with the TIP3P charges on the QM atoms, PME for MM-MM, and NVE
at 0.25 fs from 300 K velocities.
It runs the same system with every water classical as a control, and
fails unless ~md_cp2k.out~ reports the ~opencpmd~ backend.

#+begin_src bash
meson setup build-cpmd -Dwith_cpmd=true -Dcpmd_root=$CPMD_ROOT

** Stress
~tests/python/test_gromacs_opencpmd.py~ compares ~cp2k_get_stress_tensor()~
with ASE's central difference of the library's own energy under strains of
the cell, at orbital convergence 1e-8, when ~CPMDC_GROMACS_LIB~ is an
OpenCPMD build and ~CPMDC_PSEUDO_DIR~ is set.
With ~CELL PERIODIC~ (~tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt~) the
strain is 1e-6: each strained cell is a new setup at the deck's cutoff, and
in the triclinic cell a strain of 1e-4 already takes plane waves across the
cutoff.
With ~CELL NONE~ and the mechanical embedding the strain is 1e-5.

**** Before recentring
The first run of these commands, on a library that kept the QM region's
absolute position in its cell, gave the QM water 844.6 +- 14.3 kg/m^3
(2.2761 +- 0.0390 nm^3, -337 +- 171 bar, 5.7 ps discarded): its box held
1.98 to 2.08 nm^3 for 6 ps, then grew to 2.5 nm^3 by 13 ps while the
pressure turned more negative, and its final QM oxygen sat 0.2 nm from a
face of the 1 nm cluster cell.
A water diffuses half a nanometre or more in 15 ps, as the one above did.
~bench/cluster_drift_scan.py~ moves a BLYP water towards a face of the
deck's 10 Angstrom Hockney cell; ~bench/results/cluster_drift_scan.org~
has the tables.
On that library the energy moves by -3.9e-5 Hartree with the outer
hydrogen 3 Angstrom from the face, -2.8e-4 at 1 Angstrom, +5.0e-4 at
0.5 Angstrom and -8.4e-3 at 0.5 Angstrom past it, where the net force is
0.17 Hartree/Bohr.
The trace of ~sum x (x) f~ goes from +0.049 Hartree at the centre to
+0.093 at 1 Angstrom and -0.099 at 0.5 Angstrom; the 0.19 Hartree between
those two is 1300 bar on a 2.1 nm^3 box, against the -13 +- 26 bar of the
control, and past the face it is +3.2 Hartree.
The barostat scales coordinates about the origin, so a growing box also
moves a QM water that is off the origin towards a face.
With recentring every one of those positions gives the energy of the
centred water to 1.1e-14 Hartree and its forces to 3.4e-14 Hartree/Bohr,
the forces sum to zero, and the trace is 0.062214 Hartree at all of them.
