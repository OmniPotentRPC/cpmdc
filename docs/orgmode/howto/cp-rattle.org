#+TITLE: Take a Car-Parrinello step with iterative RATTLE
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:t

* Goal
Advance orbitals with velocity Verlet under the orthonormality constraints, and report the conserved energy of that step.
A host-driven force call still optimises the wavefunction.
It does not integrate a Car-Parrinello trajectory.
OpenCPMD orthonormalises a propagation with ~ortho~.
With ~LOWDIN~ set, ~lowdin~ in ~lowdin_utils~ diagonalises the overlap through ~dspevy~ and applies the symmetric factor of Löwdin (J. Chem. Phys. 18, 365 (1950), doi:10.1063/1.1747632).
Without that keyword the same routine calls Gram-Schmidt (~rgs~).
Quantum ESPRESSO ~cp.x~ selects the iterative orthonormalisation with ~orthogonalization = 'ortho'~ and warns that ~Gram-Schmidt~ yields incorrect energies and eigenvalues ([[https://www.quantum-espresso.org/Doc/INPUT_CP.html][cp.x input description]], keywords ~orthogonalization~, ~ortho_eps~, and ~ortho_max~).
CP2K's production dynamics are Born-Oppenheimer molecular dynamics and the second-generation Car-Parrinello scheme of Kühne and co-workers (J. Chem. Phys. 152, 194103 (2020), doi:10.1063/5.0007045), which keeps the electrons near the ground state with one preconditioned gradient.
The orbital extrapolation already used between force calls is that Born-Oppenheimer path.
This page is the constrained Verlet those force calls do not take.

* The step
~include/cpmdc_cp.h~ declares ~cpmdc_cp_step~.
The caller passes positions, ionic velocities, coefficient velocities, and a force callback.
Nothing in ~cpmdc_session_calculate_result()~ calls it.
~CPMDC_CP_ORTHO_LOWDIN~ builds the symmetric factor by a Jacobi diagonalisation of the overlap.
~CPMDC_CP_ORTHO_RATTLE~ solves the same constraints iteratively.
SHAKE (Ryckaert, Ciccotti, and Berendsen, J. Comput. Phys. 23, 327 (1977), doi:10.1016/0021-9991(77)90098-5) corrects the coefficients by a symmetric multiplier in the column space of the coefficients at the start of the step.
RATTLE (Andersen, J. Comput. Phys. 52, 24 (1983), doi:10.1016/0021-9991(83)90014-1) adds that multiplier to the half-step velocity and, after the second kick, removes the part of the velocity that would change ~C^T C~.
The conserved energy is the sum of the potential, the ionic kinetic energy, and the fictitious electronic kinetic energy, as in the Car-Parrinello Lagrangian (Car and Parrinello, Phys. Rev. Lett. 55, 2471 (1985), doi:10.1103/PhysRevLett.55.2471; Pastore, Smargiassi, and Buda, Phys. Rev. A 44, 6334 (1991), doi:10.1103/PhysRevA.44.6334).
A Car-Parrinello step runs no self-consistent field iteration, so the SCF count on this path is zero.

* Water model used for the check
The ions sit at the geometry in ~tests/force_input_water.capnp.txt~.
One Bohr is 0.529177210903 Å.
Each atom carries one s function.
The on-site energies are -0.60 Ha on oxygen and -0.20 Ha on hydrogen.
The hopping is ~-0.25 exp(-1.50 (r - r_OH))~ Ha, with ~r_OH~ the oxygen-hydrogen distance of that geometry.
Two orbitals are occupied and the electronic energy is twice the sum of the two lowest eigenvalues.
Harmonic bonds with force constants 0.50 Ha/Bohr² (O-H) and 0.10 Ha/Bohr² (H-H) use the bond lengths of the same geometry as their rest lengths, so the molecule vibrates.
Masses are 16 u and 1 u, with 1 u = 1822.888486209 electron masses.
The fictitious electronic mass is 400 and the time step is 0.25 atomic units.
Velocities start at zero and the coefficients start as the eigenvectors of the Hamiltonian.
LAPACK ~dsyev~ gives -1.936667976738 Ha for that electronic energy.
~tests/test_cp_rattle.c~ requires the library value to agree within 1e-10 Ha, the ionic and electronic forces to agree with central differences, the orbitals to stay orthonormal to 1e-10, and an 80-step RATTLE trajectory to keep the conserved-energy drift under 2e-7 Ha and to return the ions to 1e-10 Bohr after the velocities are reversed.
That test run printed:

#+begin_src text
water_band_energy_ha=-1.936667976738
rattle_drift_ha=4.921e-08 rattle_return_rms_bohr=1.282e-16 shake_iterations=240
#+end_src

* Benchmark
~build/cp-rattle~ is compiled at optimisation level 2, with the project's ~-ffp-contract=off~.
~build/cp-rattle 2000 40~ times forty repeats of one 2000-step trajectory and reports the maximum departure of the conserved energy from its initial value on that trajectory.
Both columns below are from one run of that command.

| Path | Time per step (s) | SCF iterations | Overlap iterations per step | Max conserved-energy drift (Ha) |
|------+-------------------+----------------+-----------------------------+---------------------------------|
| spectral Löwdin | 3.090078500e-07 | 0 | 0.458500 | 4.314417233e-01 |
| iterative RATTLE | 3.601468250e-07 | 0 | 3.617000 | 5.410173338e-02 |

On this two-orbital model the Jacobi diagonalisation is the cheaper step.
The iterative constraint step is the one whose conserved energy stays closer to its initial value.
The force-call path is unchanged, and RATTLE runs only when the caller selects ~CPMDC_CP_ORTHO_RATTLE~.
