* What this page is
~bench/opencpmd_invariance/~ holds a harness that builds OpenCPMD
~062582b7cfd832d36f88f504cd08e4ead42eb404~ and compares ~cpmd.x~ across MPI
ranks and OpenMP threads.
The numbers are measurements of that OpenCPMD revision.
They are not a statement about ~cpmdc~.

* Reproduce
The inputs, the evidence patch, and the driver live in the bench directory.
Build notes, the compiler flags, and the result tables are in
~bench/opencpmd_invariance/README.org~.

#+begin_src bash
bench/opencpmd_invariance/build_opencpmd.sh stock
python3 bench/opencpmd_invariance/run_grid.py --variants stock
python3 bench/opencpmd_invariance/analyze.py
#+end_src

The patch is evidence only, not for upstream as is.
Behavior is selected by ~src/evidence_flags.h~ in the build tree.
