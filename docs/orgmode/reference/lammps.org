#+TITLE: LAMMPS interface reference
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:t

Both routes of [[file:../howto/lammps.org][Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces]],
~cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal~ and ~pair_style cpmdc~, pass the same numbers to
LAMMPS.
The Python class adapts ~cpmdc.Session~ to rgpot's Python potential protocol
and delegates the LAMMPS mechanics to ~rgpot.lammps.FixExternal~. The compiled
pair style remains cpmdc's LAMMPS-specific route.
This page gives their units, the cell and positions they send, and the virial
they return.

* Units
cpmdc works in Bohr and Hartree.
Both routes convert with the constants of ~python/cpmdc~ (CODATA 2018, with the
thermochemical calorie, 4.184 J, for ~real~):

| LAMMPS ~units~ | Length per Bohr | Energy per Hartree | Forces | Virial | Pressure |
|----------------+-----------------+--------------------+--------+----------------------+----------|
| ~metal~ | ~BOHR_TO_ANGSTROM~ Angstrom | ~HARTREE_TO_EV~ eV | eV/Angstrom | eV | bar |
| ~real~ | ~BOHR_TO_ANGSTROM~ Angstrom | ~HARTREE_TO_KCAL_PER_MOL~ kcal/mol | kcal/(mol Angstrom) | kcal/mol | atm |
| ~electron~ | 1 Bohr | 1 Hartree | Hartree/Bohr | Hartree | Pa |

Any other unit style is refused before the first step with an error that names
the three; ~FixExternal~ raises ~ValueError~.
LAMMPS converts the virial to pressure with its own ~nktv2p~.

* Cell and positions
LAMMPS keeps a restricted triclinic box: ~a~ along x, ~b~ in the xy plane. cpmdc
takes one lattice vector per row of a row-major 3 by 3 cell, so the cell sent is

| Row | LAMMPS | ~domain->h~ |
|-----+--------+-------------|
| ~a~ | ~(lx, 0, 0)~ | ~(h[0], 0, 0)~ |
| ~b~ | ~(xy, ly, 0)~ | ~(h[5], h[1], 0)~ |
| ~c~ | ~(xz, yz, lz)~ | ~(h[4], h[3], h[2])~ |

with ~lx = xhi - xlo~ and the tilt factors ~xy~, ~xz~, ~yz~.
An orthogonal box is the case with zero tilts.
The positions are LAMMPS' Cartesian coordinates unwrapped with the image flags,
~x + ix a + iy b + iz c~, so each atom follows a continuous path across the
boundaries and wavefunction extrapolation sees smooth steps.
The box origin ~(xlo, ylo, zlo)~ is not subtracted.
A box with all three axes periodic sends the cell; a box with none sends no
cell, and the ~CPMDParams~ deck sets the box.
A box with some axes periodic and others not is refused; ~FixExternal~ raises
~ValueError~.

* Virial and pressure
cpmdc returns the virial ~W = -dE/d(strain)~ in Hartree.
LAMMPS' global virial of a pair style or of ~fix_modify virial yes~ has the same
sign and units of energy, ~W = sum r f~ for a pair potential, so both routes
pass ~W~ times the energy factor, symmetrized, as ~xx yy zz xy xz yz~.
The pressure LAMMPS prints is then ~P = (sum m v v + W) / V~ in its pressure
units; a compressed cell has a positive diagonal of ~W~ and a positive pressure.
~fix npt~ and ~fix nph~ use it as they use any pair virial.
~compute pressure NULL virial~ reports the virial part alone, ~W / V~ in
pressure units.
