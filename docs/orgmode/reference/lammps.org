#+TITLE: LAMMPS interface reference
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:t

Both routes of [[file:../howto/lammps.org][Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces]],
~cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal~ and ~pair_style cpmdc~, pass the same numbers to
LAMMPS.
The Python class adapts ~cpmdc.Session~ to the standard rgpot Python potential
protocol and delegates its LAMMPS mechanics to ~rgpot.lammps.FixExternal~.
The plugin remains cpmdc's compiled, LAMMPS-specific route.
This page gives their units, the cell and positions they send, and the virial
they return.

* Units
cpmdc works in Bohr and Hartree.
Both routes convert with the constants of ~python/cpmdc~ (CODATA 2018, with the
thermochemical calorie, 4.184 J, for ~real~):

| LAMMPS ~units~ | Length per Bohr | Energy per Hartree | Forces | Virial | Pressure |
|----------------+-----------------+--------------------+--------+----------------------+----------|
| ~metal~ | ~BOHR_TO_ANGSTROM~ Angstrom | ~HARTREE_TO_EV~ eV | eV/Angstrom | eV | bar |
| ~real~ | ~BOHR_TO_ANGSTROM~ Angstrom | ~HARTREE_TO_KCAL_PER_MOL~ kcal/mol | kcal/(mol Angstrom) | kcal/mol | atm |
| ~electron~ | 1 Bohr | 1 Hartree | Hartree/Bohr | Hartree | Pa |

Any other unit style is refused before the first step with an error that names
the three; ~FixExternal~ raises ~ValueError~.
LAMMPS converts the virial to pressure with its own ~nktv2p~.

* Cell and positions
LAMMPS keeps a restricted triclinic box: ~a~ along x, ~b~ in the xy plane. cpmdc
takes one lattice vector per row of a row-major 3 by 3 cell, so the cell sent is

| Row | LAMMPS | ~domain->h~ |
|-----+--------+-------------|
| ~a~ | ~(lx, 0, 0)~ | ~(h[0], 0, 0)~ |
| ~b~ | ~(xy, ly, 0)~ | ~(h[5], h[1], 0)~ |
| ~c~ | ~(xz, yz, lz)~ | ~(h[4], h[3], h[2])~ |

with ~lx = xhi - xlo~ and the tilt factors ~xy~, ~xz~, ~yz~.
An orthogonal box is the case with zero tilts.
The positions are LAMMPS' Cartesian coordinates unwrapped with the image flags,
~x + ix a + iy b + iz c~, so each atom follows a continuous path across the
boundaries and wavefunction extrapolation sees smooth steps.
The box origin ~(xlo, ylo, zlo)~ is not subtracted.
A box with all three axes periodic sends the cell; a box with none sends no
cell, and the ~CPMDParams~ deck sets the box.
A box with some axes periodic and others not is refused; ~FixExternal~ raises
~ValueError~.

* Atom IDs
Both routes gather the system and order their call to cpmdc by increasing atom
ID.
IDs must be positive and unique, but need not be the consecutive integers 1
through N: deleting atoms without compression leaves holes that are preserved
while each force is returned to its original atom.

Changing the atoms' elemental composition, including by
~delete_atoms ... compress no~ or ~fix evaporate~, closes the current cpmdc
session before the next evaluation and opens a new session for the changed
topology.
An unchanged composition continues on the existing session.

* Electrostatic QM/MM embedding
Both routes keep their whole-system behavior by default.
For ~FixExternal~, pass ~qm_types=[...]~; other atom types provide their LAMMPS
~q~ values as Gaussian MM charges.
~mm_width_angstrom~ defaults to 0.30.
For the plugin, write ~NULL~ for each MM type, for example
~pair_style cpmdc params.bin width 0.30~ followed by ~pair_coeff * * O H NULL~.

Element entries are QM and ~NULL~ entries use the per-atom charge.
The energy includes the QM--MM electrostatic coupling and forces are returned to
both groups.
Electrostatic embedding is currently isolated only; a periodic box is rejected
instead of using an incomplete image convention, so the virial
\(-dE/d(\mathrm{strain})\), which needs a cell, is zero on both routes.
~qm_types~ must name at least one type, and ~mm_width_angstrom~ and the plugin's
~width~ must be finite and at least 1e-12 Angstrom; a smaller value is refused
when the fix or the pair style is configured.
Both routes send the charges on every step that has MM atoms, and clear them on
the first step that has none after a step that had some, so deleting or retyping
every MM atom leaves no stale charge in the session.

* Virial and pressure
cpmdc returns the virial ~W = -dE/d(strain)~ in Hartree.
LAMMPS' global virial of a pair style or of ~fix_modify virial yes~ has the same
sign and units of energy, ~W = sum r f~ for a pair potential, so both routes
pass ~W~ times the energy factor, symmetrized, as ~xx yy zz xy xz yz~.
The pressure LAMMPS prints is then ~P = (sum m v v + W) / V~ in its pressure
units; a compressed cell has a positive diagonal of ~W~ and a positive pressure.
~fix npt~ and ~fix nph~ use it as they use any pair virial.
~compute ID all pressure NULL virial~ reports the virial part alone, ~W / V~
in pressure units.
LAMMPS accepts only the group ~all~ for that compute.

* The Python session adapter
~cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal~ hands ~rgpot.lammps.FixExternal~ a small potential
that wraps one ~cpmdc.Session~.
rgpot passes Angstrom and eV, so the adapter turns them into the atomic units
~Session.calculate_au~ takes, and back, with factors it derives once when the
driver is built, from the unit factors of rgpot and of this page:

- Positions and the cell are divided by one factor, the LAMMPS length per Bohr
  over the length per Angstrom of rgpot.
  Dividing, as the route before the delegation did, keeps the energy and the
  forces of ~metal~ units bit-identical to that route; multiplying by the
  reciprocal would not.
- The energy and the forces are multiplied by the energy per Hartree over the
  energy per eV of rgpot, and the forces by that factor over the length factor.
- Forces and virial are views of one buffer, so one finiteness test covers both
  and the energy.
  Only after that test fails does the adapter test each array to name the one
  that holds the non-finite value; the message is
  ~the library returned a non-finite energy~, ~forces~, or ~virial~.
- The composition guard compares the sorted atomic numbers as plain integers.
  A changed composition closes the session and opens a new one, as above.
- The stress rgpot expects is ~-W / V~ in the energy factor.
  ~V~ is the absolute determinant of the 3 by 3 cell, written out as its six
  products, not a linear algebra call.
  In ~metal~ units the virial agrees with the earlier route to round-off, not
  bit for bit, because rgpot multiplies the stress by its own determinant of the
  cell.

rgpot may do per-step work of its own on its side of this protocol.
The adapter does not depend on it, and any ~rgpot.lammps~ that provides
~unit_factors~, ~length_per_angstrom~ and ~energy_per_ev~ works.

* Per-atom quantities
Forces are per atom, and both routes return them for every atom.
The energy and the virial are totals of the whole system, and ~pair_style cpmdc~
splits neither:

- A density functional energy is a functional of the electron density, not a sum
  of terms that belong to atoms, so any per-atom share of it would be a choice
  of partition, not a result of cpmdc.
- The virial ~-dE/d(strain)~ is a derivative with respect to the cell.
  It holds kinetic, Hartree, exchange-correlation, and basis-set (Pulay) parts
  that are not ~sum r f~ over atoms, so it has no per-atom form that adds up to
  the pressure.

~pair_style cpmdc~ therefore refuses a run in which a compute asks for a split:
~compute pe/atom~, ~compute stress/atom~, or ~compute centroid/stress/atom~,
with any keywords, even one that is never printed.
The error
~pair_style cpmdc computes no per-atom energy or virial (compute ID)~ names the compute and comes from ~init_style~,
before the first evaluation.
~uncompute ID~ removes the request.
~FixExternal~ hands LAMMPS one energy and one virial and sets no per-atom
values, so ~compute pe/atom~ and ~compute stress/atom~ take nothing from it.
Per-atom forces work with every compute, for example
~compute ID group-ID property/atom fx fy fz~.
