#+TITLE: cpmdc inside MD engines
#+OPTIONS: toc:nil todo:nil num:nil author:nil title:t

This page explains why GROMACS reaches cpmdc through its CP2K
QM/MM interface, reports runs of that route on OpenCPMD, and compares the cost per step of the LAMMPS and GROMACS routes. The steps are in [[file:../howto/gromacs.org][Run GROMACS on cpmdc forces]] and [[file:../howto/lammps.org][Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces]]; the conventions are in the [[file:../reference/gromacs.org][GROMACS]] and [[file:../reference/lammps.org][LAMMPS]]
interface references.

* Why the GROMACS CP2K interface
GROMACS offers four ways to bring outside forces into ~mdrun~.
The choice was made from the GROMACS 2024 to 2026 sources and manuals and the
MiMiC papers:

| Route | Coupling | What rules it in or out |
|-------+----------+--------------------------|
| CP2K QM/MM interface (~GMX_CP2K~) | in process, one C call per step | GROMACS integrates; energy enters ~Quantum En.~ (~F_EQM~) and so the potential and the conserved energy; positions, box, and forces in double precision in a double build. Chosen |
| MiMiC (~GMX_MIMIC~, ~integrator = mimic~) | two MPI programs over the MiMiC communication library | built for CPMD, but CPMD is the server that integrates and GROMACS a client that returns MM forces (~src/gromacs/mimic/communicator.cpp~); electrostatic embedding is computed against CPMD's density on its grid. A MiMiC client of cpmdc would need CPMD's integrator and MiMiC's embedding inside a session, which the C ABI does not expose, and GROMACS' ~mimic~ integrator reports no kinetic or total energy |
| neural-network potential (~GMX_NNPOT~, 2025 and later) | in process, a TorchScript model | ~src/gromacs/applied_forces/nnpot/torchmodel.cpp~ casts positions and box to ~float32~, so no model can match a double reference to 1e-10; it adds no virial |
| PLUMED (native in 2025) | in process | the bias forces and virial reach GROMACS, but ~plumedforceprovider.cpp~ never adds the bias energy to GROMACS' energy terms, so the potential and NVE checks would miss it |
| i-PI | socket | GROMACS has no i-PI client |

MiMiC couples CPMD to GROMACS with electrostatic embedding and scales to
thousands of cores [1, 2], through the MiMiC communication library [3]; it is
the route for an MPI-launched CPMD that integrates.
For GROMACS integrating on cpmdc forces, the CP2K interface is the one
in-process route that carries the energy, the forces, and the box at full
precision, and it is the one built and tested here.

* OpenCPMD in GROMACS
~bench/gromacs_opencpmd.py~ runs GROMACS on the OpenCPMD backend: one BLYP water (the deck of ~tests/water_blyp_params.capnp.txt~,
50 Ry, Hockney, orbital convergence 1e-7) at the centre of the deck's 1 nm cell,
among 26 flexible TIP3P waters in a 1.8 nm box, with ~CELL NONE~, mechanical
embedding with the TIP3P charges on the QM atoms, PME for MM-MM, and NVE at 0.25
fs from 300 K velocities.
It runs the same system with every water classical as a control, and fails
unless ~md_cp2k.out~ reports the ~opencpmd~ backend.

#+begin_src bash
meson setup build-cpmd -Dwith_cpmd=true -Dcpmd_root=$CPMD_ROOT
meson compile -C build-cpmd
scripts/ci/build_gromacs_cpmdc.sh 2025.5 $GMX_PREFIX build-cpmd/tools/gromacs mpi
python bench/gromacs_opencpmd.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --pseudo-dir $CPMD_ROOT/../Regtests/tests/PP_LIBRARY --ranks 4,1 --steps 200
#+end_src

One run on an 8-core Xeon virtual machine, GROMACS 2025.5 (double, Open MPI
5.0.10, OpenBLAS), OpenCPMD with Open MPI, ~QM_RANKS ALL~, 200 steps (50 fs);
the 4-rank run shared the machine with other jobs:

| Run | Drift, kJ/mol/ps | Per atom | Largest excursion, kJ/mol | Kinetic energy std, kJ/mol | Wall, s |
|-----+------------------+----------+---------------------------+----------------------------+---------|
| OpenCPMD QM water, 4 ranks | 0.768 | 0.00948 | 1.274 | 28.81 | 1079.5 |
| OpenCPMD QM water, 1 rank | 0.768 | 0.00948 | 1.274 | 28.81 | 2194.0 |
| all classical, 1 rank | 0.697 | 0.00861 | 1.292 | 28.94 | |

The step-0 QM energy is -17.055716980795 Hartree; ~CPMDCCalculator~ gives
-17.05571697 for the same water.
The total energy oscillates by about 1.3 kJ/mol as 105 kJ/mol of kinetic energy
goes into the flexible O-H bonds; the classical control shows the same, and
halving the time step divides it by four, so it is leap-frog's error, not the QM
forces'.
Over 50 fs the QM water adds 0.07 kJ/mol/ps to the drift of the control.

Between rank counts the QM energy at step 0 agrees to 1.3e-9
kJ/mol and the total energy over the run to 2.4e-5 kJ/mol, but the QM atom
forces differ by 1e-5 kJ/mol/nm at step 0 and 2e-3 at step 200, about 2e-6 of
the largest force: OpenCPMD distributes its plane waves over the ranks, its sums
run in a different order, and its SCF stops at a slightly different point.
A tighter orbital convergence does not shrink that difference: at 1e-9 the
step-0 oxygen forces of 1 and 2 ranks differ by 5e-4 kJ/mol/nm.
With ~QM_RANKS 1~ the QM calculation runs on rank 0 alone, and 4 ranks match 1
rank to 3e-13 kJ/mol/nm in the forces and 7e-12 kJ/mol in the total energy over
10 steps, at the cost of the SCF time of one rank.
For 1e-10 agreement with a one-rank run, use ~QM_RANKS 1~.

** Stress
~tests/python/test_gromacs_opencpmd.py~ compares
~cp2k_get_stress_tensor()~ with ASE's central difference of the library's own energy under strains of the cell, at orbital convergence 1e-8, when ~CPMDC_GROMACS_LIB~ is an OpenCPMD build and ~CPMDC_PSEUDO_DIR~
is set.
With ~CELL PERIODIC~ (~tests/water_blyp_periodic_params.capnp.txt~) the strain
is 1e-6: each strained cell is a new setup at the deck's cutoff, and in the
triclinic cell a strain of 1e-4 already takes plane waves across the cutoff.
With ~CELL NONE~ and the mechanical embedding the strain is 1e-5.
One run, 1 rank, in eV/Angstrom^3:

| Case | Largest stress element | Largest difference | Ratio |
|------+------------------------+--------------------+-------|
| ~CELL PERIODIC~, 1 nm cube | 1.889e-2 | 9.703e-9 | 5.1e-7 |
| ~CELL PERIODIC~, triclinic | 1.927e-2 | 7.553e-9 | 3.9e-7 |
| ~CELL NONE~ in TIP3P, cube | 2.545e-4 | 5.875e-10 | 2.3e-6 |
| ~CELL NONE~ in TIP3P, triclinic | 2.545e-4 | 3.096e-9 | 1.2e-5 |

The test allows 1e-6 of the largest element with ~CELL PERIODIC~, where the
stress also matches ~CPMDCCalculator~ to 1e-7, and 3e-5 with ~CELL NONE~, where
the difference shrinks as the strain squared.

** NPT
~bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py~ runs NPT on the system above, v-rescale at 300 K
and isotropic C-rescale (or ~--pcoupl Parrinello-Rahman~) at 1 bar with ~tau-p~
0.5 ps, compressibility 4.5e-5
/bar, and 0.5 fs steps, once with the QM water and once all classical. The deck predicts the orbitals with ASPC of order 4 at orbital convergence 1e-6. ~--lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize~ replaces the 27 waters at 0.14 g/cm^3
by 64 at 1.00 g/cm^3 in a 1.24 nm box, relaxed classically first:

#+begin_src bash
python bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --ranks 8 --pseudo-dir $CPMD_ROOT/../Regtests/tests/PP_LIBRARY --steps 30000 \
  --lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize --workdir npt --json npt.json
python bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --classical-only --steps 2000000 --lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize \
  --workdir npt-long --json npt-long.json
python bench/npt_block_average.py npt.json --reference npt-long.json \
  --output bench/results/gromacs_npt_crescale_64w.org
#+end_src

The first command writes the energy frames of both runs, every 10 steps, to
~npt.json~; the second runs the classical control alone for 1 ns, which takes
minutes; the third discards each run's equilibration, block-averages the rest,
and writes the tables below.
The JSON files and the run directories stay out of the repository; for the run
below, ~bench/results/gromacs_npt_crescale_64w_blocks.csv~ and
~_windows.csv~ hold the block averages and window means at full precision, and ~_runs.json~
the per-run summaries and arguments of both JSON files without their frames or
paths.

The classical box has settled: over 1 ns its pressure, -13 +- 26 bar, matches
the 1 bar of the barostat, and its density, 918.5 +- 3.4
kg/m^3, is that of this flexible TIP3P with 0.55 nm cut-offs in a 64-water box. Its blocking error still grows from 20 to 82 ps blocks, so that error is a lower bound too; it puts the statistical inefficiency of the volume at 7.2 ps, and the volume of 64 waters fluctuates by 0.09 nm^3, so a 15 ps run holds few independent samples. The 15 ps control, 906.3 +- 7.7 kg/m^3,
is 1.5 errors from it; its 1 ps window means of the volume span 0.20 nm^3 from 2
ps on.

The QM run, 931.4 +- 7.3 kg/m^3, is 1.6 combined errors above the 1 ns control.
From 2 ps on its 1 ps window means of the volume span 0.17 nm^3, 1.99 to 2.16
nm^3, without a trend: the last window, 2.078 nm^3, lies inside the range of the
first ones, and the box ends at 2.044 nm^3.
Its blocking error, like that of the 15 ps control, still grows at the largest
block, so it is a lower bound, and the mean pressure, 310 +- 128 bar, is 2.4 of
those errors from 1 bar.
The barostat follows the pressure GROMACS reports: the least-squares slope of
the change of ln V from one frame to the next against the pressure is 1.049
times compressibility x 5 fs / ~tau-p~ with the QM water and 1.005 times in the
control.
From 1 ps on, the 1 ps window means of ~Quantum En.~ lie within 15.5
kJ/mol of each other, without a step. GROMACS' conserved energy drifts by 191.5 kJ/mol/ps
with the QM water and 181.5 in the control, so the drift is not from the QM
water.
The final ~md.gro~ puts the QM oxygen at (0.511, 0.455, 0.892) nm, 0.44 nm from
where it started after the minimisation, with its nearest oxygens 0.269, 0.281
and 0.299 nm away, against 0.274, 0.283 and 0.304 nm for the same water in the
control.

**** Before recentring
The first run of these commands, on a library that kept the QM region's absolute
position in its cell, gave the QM water 844.6 +- 14.3 kg/m^3 (2.2761 +- 0.0390
nm^3, -337 +- 171 bar, 5.7 ps discarded): its box held 1.98 to 2.08 nm^3 for 6
ps, then grew to 2.5 nm^3 by 13 ps while the pressure turned more negative, and
its final QM oxygen sat 0.2 nm from a face of the 1 nm cluster cell.
A water diffuses half a nanometre or more in 15 ps, as the one above did.

~bench/cluster_drift_scan.py~ moves a BLYP water towards a face of the deck's 10 Angstrom Hockney cell; ~bench/results/cluster_drift_scan.org~
has the tables.
On that library the energy moves by -3.9e-5 Hartree with the outer hydrogen 3
Angstrom from the face, -2.8e-4 at 1 Angstrom, +5.0e-4 at 0.5 Angstrom and
-8.4e-3 at 0.5 Angstrom past it, where the net force is 0.17 Hartree/Bohr.
The trace of ~sum x (x) f~ goes from +0.049 Hartree at the centre to +0.093 at 1
Angstrom and -0.099 at 0.5 Angstrom; the 0.19 Hartree between those two is 1300
bar on a 2.1 nm^3 box, against the -13 +- 26 bar of the control, and past the
face it is +3.2 Hartree.
The barostat scales coordinates about the origin, so a growing box also moves a
QM water that is off the origin towards a face.
With recentring every one of those positions gives the energy of the centred
water to 1.1e-14 Hartree and its forces to 3.4e-14 Hartree/Bohr, the forces sum
to zero, and the trace is 0.062214 Hartree at all of them.

* Overhead per step
** LAMMPS routes
~bench/lammps_routes.py~ integrates two water molecules in a 9 Angstrom cubic
box (~metal~, NVE) through each route and reports the median wall time per step
over fresh repeats, with the raw values.
With the reference evaluator this is coupling overhead: an OpenCPMD step adds
the same SCF time to every route.

#+begin_src bash
python bench/lammps_routes.py --build build --steps 2000 --repeats 7
#+end_src

One run on an 8-core Xeon virtual machine with LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 from the pip
wheel and i-PI 3.3.1:

| Route | Median, microseconds per step |
|-------+-------------------------------|
| ~Session.calculate_au~ in a Python loop, no integrator | 7.4 |
| LAMMPS with ~pair_style zero~, no cpmdc | 0.65 |
| ~pair_style cpmdc~ | 7.1 |
| ~FixExternal~ | 87 |
| ~FixExternal~ through ASE's ~SocketServer~ and ~cpmdc-ipi-c~ | 253 |
| i-PI with ~cpmdc-ipi-c~ | 231 |
| i-PI with ~cpmdc-ipi-c~ and LAMMPS ~fix ipi~ | 318 |

The in-process routes time ~run~ inside one process; the i-PI routes use i-PI's
own per-step timing, so no route counts process startup.
The GROMACS table below times GROMACS on the same system beside
~pair_style cpmdc~.

** GROMACS routes
~bench/gromacs_routes.py~ integrates the system of ~bench/lammps_routes.py~, two
water molecules in a 0.9 nm cube on the reference evaluator with the periodic
deck, NVE at 0.25 fs, through GROMACS and through LAMMPS.
GROMACS runs twice, for 1000 and 1000 + ~STEPS~ steps, and the per-step time is
the difference of md.log's wall times over ~STEPS~, so startup and the first
evaluation cancel.

#+begin_src bash
CPMDC_GMX=gmx_d python bench/gromacs_routes.py --build build --steps 20000 --repeats 7
#+end_src

one rank, one OpenMP thread) and LAMMPS 22 Jul 2025 from the pip wheel, 20000
steps, 7 repeats:

| ~Session.calculate_au~ in a Python loop, no integrator | 5.95 | 6.00 5.91 5.92 5.91 6.05 6.02 5.95 |
| LAMMPS with ~pair_style zero~, no cpmdc | 0.48 | 0.48 0.49 0.49 0.48 0.50 0.48 0.48 |
| LAMMPS with ~pair_style cpmdc~ | 10.43 | 10.46 10.45 10.43 10.41 10.46 10.42 10.40 |
