The session adapter behind `cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal` does less work on each LAMMPS step. It derives its unit factors once when the driver is built, divides positions and the cell by one Angstrom per Bohr factor, screens forces and virial with one finiteness check that shares a buffer, names the offending array only after that check fails, and takes the cell volume from the explicit 3 by 3 determinant instead of a linear algebra call. In LAMMPS `metal` units the energy and forces are bit-identical to the route before the delegation to `rgpot.lammps`, and the virial agrees to round-off.
