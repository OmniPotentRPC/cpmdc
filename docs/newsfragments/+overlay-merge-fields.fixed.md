Merging a `common` overlay (`CommonMethodSpec`) into a native `cpmd` arm in `cpmdc_configure()`, `cpmdc_session_create_from_config()`, `cpmdc_session_configure()`, and `cpmdc_calculate_result_from_config()` now validates `basisSet` (`"planewave"` or unset), lowers Fermi `smearing` into `&CPMD` when free-energy controls are absent, copies `vanDerWaalsS6` into a `vdwParams` section when `s6` is unset, and rejects conflicting, unmappable, or out-of-range overlay values through `cpmdc_last_error()`. Overlay-only lowering also rejects `vanDerWaalsS6` without `vanDerWaalsMethod` and non-zero `smearing.widthEv` with `kind = none`.
