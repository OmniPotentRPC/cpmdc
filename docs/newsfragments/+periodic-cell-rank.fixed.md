`CPMDCCalculator` and `cpmdc-ipi` accept a periodic cell when the singular values of its 3 by 3 matrix exceed the largest times 3 times the float64 unit roundoff, the cutoff `numpy.linalg.matrix_rank` uses. The previous test compared the cell volume with a fixed fraction of the product of the edge lengths. An i-PI `INIT` whose `batch_size` is not the integer 1, and a negative `--wait`, are reported instead of being treated as the ordinary case.
