A session can apply Gaussian-smeared MM point charges to the CPMD density and returns the forces on both the QM atoms and the MM charges, through `cpmdc_session_set_external_charges()` and `cpmdc_session_last_external_charge_forces()`. The energy includes the electron and ion coupling to the charges, and both force sets are `-dE/dR`. The potential uses a Taylor series near a charge centre, so the energy and forces stay finite where a charge sits on an ion. A Gaussian width must be finite and at least 1e-12 Angstrom, and a narrower one is refused with `CPMDC_ERROR_INVALID_ARGUMENT` before it can overflow. The system must be isolated: a cell, or a deck with a symmetry above 0, a surface, or a polymer, is refused with `CPMDC_ERROR_UNSUPPORTED_PERIODIC_EMBEDDING` on every evaluation path. GROMACS takes `EMBEDDING ELECTROSTATIC` with `MM_WIDTH` in nm, `cpmdc.lammps.FixExternal` takes `qm_types` and `mm_width_angstrom`, and the LAMMPS pair style takes `NULL` entries in `pair_coeff` and a `width` setting. The LAMMPS routes clear the charges on the first step that has no MM atom. The OpenCPMD patch set gains `opencpmd_external_charges.patch`.
