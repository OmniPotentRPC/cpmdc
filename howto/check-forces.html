Check forces and stress by finite differences¶

Goal¶

Compare the forces a session returns with central differences of its energy, the way the CP2K DEBUG run type does with DEBUG_FORCES and DEBUG_STRESS_TENSOR. A mismatch points at the gradient, not at the energy.

Run the check¶

cpmdc-fd-check is built with the tests (-Dwith_tests=true) and is not installed. It takes one CPMDParams message and one ForceInput step:

build/cpmdc-fd-check build/water_blyp_params.bin build/force_input_water.bin

Each coordinate moves by +DX and -DX Bohr, so a check of N atoms costs 6N energy evaluations after the first one. Every evaluation is a cpmdc_session_calculate_result() call with lengthUnit = "bohr" and energyUnit = "hartree". The numerical force is -(E(+DX) - E(-DX)) / (2 DX).

A component whose analytic value is below --eps-no-error-check is printed and not checked. A force at or below 1e-4 Hartree/Bohr may be off by five times the limit. The last line of each block gives the largest absolute and relative error and how many components were checked. The exit status is 0 when every checked component agrees, 1 on a mismatch, and 2 on a usage, input, or evaluation error.

Check the stress¶

--stress needs a periodic cell for which the calculator writes PotentialResult.stress; an isolated cell exits with status 2. Each of the six symmetric strains eps_ab is applied as +DX and -DX to the cell vectors and to the positions, so the fractional coordinates stay fixed. That costs 12 more evaluations. The check compares virials in Hartree, as CP2K compares pv_virial, so --eps-no-error-check means the same as there. The numerical virial is -(E(+DX) - E(-DX)) / (2 DX) and the analytic one is the volume times PotentialResult.stress in Hartree/Bohr3, the sign of OpenCPMD paiu/omega.

What the reference build shows¶

The reference evaluator returns the analytic gradient of its energy, so its forces agree to rounding. Its stress is the energy divided by the volume on the diagonal, which is not a strain derivative, so --stress reports a mismatch there. That stress only exists to carry a tensor through PotentialResult.

With OpenCPMD¶

An SCF energy carries the error of its convergence. Converge the orbitals well below DX times the force you want to check, or the differences measure the SCF threshold instead of the gradient.
