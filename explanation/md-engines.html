cpmdc inside MD engines¶

This page explains why GROMACS reaches cpmdc through its CP2K QM/MM interface, reports runs of that route on OpenCPMD, and compares the cost per step of the LAMMPS and GROMACS routes. The steps are in Run GROMACS on cpmdc forces and Run LAMMPS on cpmdc forces; the conventions are in the GROMACS and LAMMPS interface references.

Why the GROMACS CP2K interface¶

GROMACS offers four ways to bring outside forces into mdrun. The choice was made from the GROMACS 2024 to 2026 sources and manuals and the MiMiC papers:

Route

Coupling

What rules it in or out

CP2K QM/MM interface (GMX_CP2K)

in process, one C call per step

GROMACS integrates; energy enters Quantum En. (F_EQM) and so the potential and the conserved energy; positions, box, and forces in double precision in a double build. Chosen

MiMiC (GMX_MIMIC, integrator = mimic)

two MPI programs over the MiMiC communication library

built for CPMD, but CPMD is the server that integrates and GROMACS a client that returns MM forces (src/gromacs/mimic/communicator.cpp); electrostatic embedding is computed against CPMD’s density on its grid. A MiMiC client of cpmdc would need CPMD’s integrator and MiMiC’s embedding inside a session, which the C ABI does not expose, and GROMACS’ mimic integrator reports no kinetic or total energy

neural-network potential (GMX_NNPOT, 2025 and later)

in process, a TorchScript model

src/gromacs/applied_forces/nnpot/torchmodel.cpp casts positions and box to float32, so no model can match a double reference to 1e-10; it adds no virial

PLUMED (native in 2025)

in process

the bias forces and virial reach GROMACS, but plumedforceprovider.cpp never adds the bias energy to GROMACS’ energy terms, so the potential and NVE checks would miss it

i-PI

socket

GROMACS has no i-PI client

MiMiC couples CPMD to GROMACS with electrostatic embedding and scales to thousands of cores [1, 2], through the MiMiC communication library [3]; it is the route for an MPI-launched CPMD that integrates. For GROMACS integrating on cpmdc forces, the CP2K interface is the one in-process route that carries the energy, the forces, and the box at full precision, and it is the one built and tested here.

OpenCPMD in GROMACS¶

bench/gromacs_opencpmd.py runs GROMACS on the OpenCPMD backend: one BLYP water (the deck of tests/water_blyp_params.capnp.txt, 50 Ry, Hockney, orbital convergence 1e-7) at the centre of the deck’s 1 nm cell, among 26 flexible TIP3P waters in a 1.8 nm box, with CELL NONE, mechanical embedding with the TIP3P charges on the QM atoms, PME for MM-MM, and NVE at 0.25 fs from 300 K velocities. It runs the same system with every water classical as a control, and fails unless md_cp2k.out reports the opencpmd backend.

meson setup build-cpmd -Dwith_cpmd=true -Dcpmd_root=$CPMD_ROOT
meson compile -C build-cpmd
scripts/ci/build_gromacs_cpmdc.sh 2025.5 $GMX_PREFIX build-cpmd/tools/gromacs mpi
python bench/gromacs_opencpmd.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --pseudo-dir $CPMD_ROOT/../Regtests/tests/PP_LIBRARY --ranks 4,1 --steps 200

The step-0 QM energy is -17.055716980795 Hartree; CPMDCCalculator gives -17.05571697 for the same water. The total energy oscillates by about 1.3 kJ/mol as 105 kJ/mol of kinetic energy goes into the flexible O-H bonds; the classical control shows the same, and halving the time step divides it by four, so it is leap-frog’s error, not the QM forces’. Over 50 fs the QM water adds 0.07 kJ/mol/ps to the drift of the control.

Between rank counts the QM energy at step 0 agrees to 1.3e-9 kJ/mol and the total energy over the run to 2.4e-5 kJ/mol, but the QM atom forces differ by 1e-5 kJ/mol/nm at step 0 and 2e-3 at step 200, about 2e-6 of the largest force: OpenCPMD distributes its plane waves over the ranks, its sums run in a different order, and its SCF stops at a slightly different point. A tighter orbital convergence does not shrink that difference: at 1e-9 the step-0 oxygen forces of 1 and 2 ranks differ by 5e-4 kJ/mol/nm. With QM_RANKS 1 the QM calculation runs on rank 0 alone, and 4 ranks match 1 rank to 3e-13 kJ/mol/nm in the forces and 7e-12 kJ/mol in the total energy over 10 steps, at the cost of the SCF time of one rank. For 1e-10 agreement with a one-rank run, use QM_RANKS 1.

Stress¶

tests/python/test_gromacs_opencpmd.py compares cp2k_get_stress_tensor() with ASE’s central difference of the library’s own energy under strains of the cell, at orbital convergence 1e-8, when CPMDC_GROMACS_LIB is an OpenCPMD build and CPMDC_PSEUDO_DIR is set. With CELL NONE and the mechanical embedding the strain is 1e-5. One run, 1 rank, in eV/Angstrom3:

NPT¶

bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py runs NPT on the system above, v-rescale at 300 K and isotropic C-rescale (or --pcoupl Parrinello-Rahman) at 1 bar with tau-p 0.5 ps, compressibility 4.5e-5 /bar, and 0.5 fs steps, once with the QM water and once all classical. The deck predicts the orbitals with ASPC of order 4 at orbital convergence 1e-6. --lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize replaces the 27 waters at 0.14 g/cm3 by 64 at 1.00 g/cm3 in a 1.24 nm box, relaxed classically first:

python bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --ranks 8 --pseudo-dir $CPMD_ROOT/../Regtests/tests/PP_LIBRARY --steps 30000 \
  --lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize --workdir npt --json npt.json
python bench/gromacs_opencpmd_npt.py --gmx $GMX_PREFIX/bin/gmx_mpi_d --mpiexec mpiexec \
  --classical-only --steps 2000000 --lattice 4 --spacing 0.31 --minimize \
  --workdir npt-long --json npt-long.json
python bench/npt_block_average.py npt.json --reference npt-long.json \
  --output bench/results/gromacs_npt_crescale_64w.org

The first command writes the energy frames of both runs, every 10 steps, to npt.json; the second runs the classical control alone for 1 ns, which takes minutes; the third discards each run’s equilibration, block-averages the rest, and writes the tables below. The JSON files and the run directories stay out of the repository; for the run below, bench/results/gromacs_npt_crescale_64w_blocks.csv and _windows.csv hold the block averages and window means at full precision, and _runs.json the per-run summaries and arguments of both JSON files without their frames or paths.

The classical box has settled: over 1 ns its pressure, -13 +- 26 bar, matches the 1 bar of the barostat, and its density, 918.5 +- 3.4 kg/m3, is that of this flexible TIP3P with 0.55 nm cut-offs in a 64-water box. Its blocking error still grows from 20 to 82 ps blocks, so that error is a lower bound too; it puts the statistical inefficiency of the volume at 7.2 ps, and the volume of 64 waters fluctuates by 0.09 nm3, so a 15 ps run holds few independent samples. The 15 ps control, 906.3 +- 7.7 kg/m3, is 1.5 errors from it; its 1 ps window means of the volume span 0.20 nm3 from 2 ps on.

The QM run, 931.4 +- 7.3 kg/m3, is 1.6 combined errors above the 1 ns control. From 2 ps on its 1 ps window means of the volume span 0.17 nm3, 1.99 to 2.16 nm3, without a trend: the last window, 2.078 nm3, lies inside the range of the first ones, and the box ends at 2.044 nm3. Its blocking error, like that of the 15 ps control, still grows at the largest block, so it is a lower bound, and the mean pressure, 310 +- 128 bar, is 2.4 of those errors from 1 bar. The barostat follows the pressure GROMACS reports: the least-squares slope of the change of ln V from one frame to the next against the pressure is 1.049 times compressibility x 5 fs / tau-p with the QM water and 1.005 times in the control. From 1 ps on, the 1 ps window means of Quantum En. lie within 15.5 kJ/mol of each other, without a step. GROMACS’ conserved energy drifts by 191.5 kJ/mol/ps with the QM water and 181.5 in the control, so the drift is not from the QM water. The final md.gro puts the QM oxygen at (0.511, 0.455, 0.892) nm, 0.44 nm from where it started after the minimisation, with its nearest oxygens 0.269, 0.281 and 0.299 nm away, against 0.274, 0.283 and 0.304 nm for the same water in the control.

Before recentring¶

The first run of these commands, on a library that kept the QM region’s absolute position in its cell, gave the QM water 844.6 +- 14.3 kg/m3 (2.2761 +- 0.0390 nm3, -337 +- 171 bar, 5.7 ps discarded): its box held 1.98 to 2.08 nm3 for 6 ps, then grew to 2.5 nm3 by 13 ps while the pressure turned more negative, and its final QM oxygen sat 0.2 nm from a face of the 1 nm cluster cell. A water diffuses half a nanometre or more in 15 ps, as the one above did. bench/cluster_drift_scan.py moves a BLYP water towards a face of the deck’s 10 Angstrom Hockney cell; bench/results/cluster_drift_scan.org has the tables. On that library the energy moves by -3.9e-5 Hartree with the outer hydrogen 3 Angstrom from the face, -2.8e-4 at 1 Angstrom, +5.0e-4 at 0.5 Angstrom and -8.4e-3 at 0.5 Angstrom past it, where the net force is 0.17 Hartree/Bohr. The trace of sum x (x) f goes from +0.049 Hartree at the centre to +0.093 at 1 Angstrom and -0.099 at 0.5 Angstrom; the 0.19 Hartree between those two is 1300 bar on a 2.1 nm3 box, against the -13 +- 26 bar of the control, and past the face it is +3.2 Hartree. The barostat scales coordinates about the origin, so a growing box also moves a QM water that is off the origin towards a face. With recentring every one of those positions gives the energy of the centred water to 1.1e-14 Hartree and its forces to 3.4e-14 Hartree/Bohr, the forces sum to zero, and the trace is 0.062214 Hartree at all of them.
